What the scanner checks

No magic, no AI hype. Here's what happens when you upload your resume.

What gets checked

  • Keyword overlap between your resume and the job description.
  • Parseability: columns, graphics, tables — anything that breaks an ATS parser.
  • Section headers: parsers look for "Experience", "Education", "Skills".
  • Writing quality: passive voice, weak verbs, missing numbers.

How scoring works

  • Required skills in the job description count more than nice-to-haves.
  • Exact keyword matches score higher, related terms still count.
  • 80%+: likely to clear most ATS keyword filters.
  • 60-79%: close — a few edits should get you there.
  • Under 60%: gaps that need work.

What we don't promise

  • Every ATS is different (Greenhouse, Workday, etc). We can't test against all of them.
  • A good score helps, but your actual experience and writing still matter more.
  • We don't store your resume or share it.
